# Balance report: difficulty curve

> Generated by: `ferrustry-headless balance --difficulty normal --waves 20 --lang en`.

## How it is computed

- **Defence trial:** the real wave N (composition from the data, 3 spawn points like a medium map, ~30 tiles from the core) against K turrets in rings around the core; ammo is refilled every tick (an uninterrupted belt) and its use is counted. "Holds" = every enemy destroyed and the core takes no damage. The smallest K up to 48 is searched (binary search; spawn randomness can make neighbouring waves differ by a few turrets). Every trial runs on 3 spawn spreads and must hold in all. Arc towers each get 4 wind turbines on the core side (full power).
- **Arrival:** difficulty Normal (first wave 5:00, then every 2:00) with constant noise.
- **Production cell** of the megabase (2 bores on ore → smelter on 2 wind turbines → belt): 226 ingots per 10 min, noise 2, costs 85 ingots — measured in steady state.
- **Cells needed:** the smallest number of cells whose output by the wave's arrival pays for themselves, for the guns that hold this wave and for all ammo up to it, minus the 300 starter ingots — and their noise speeds the waves up. Optimistic: production from the first second, no logistics to the turrets.
